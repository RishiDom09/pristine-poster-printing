About us

Print your visions

Pristine Poster Printing was started by students who wanted an easy, affordable way to get custom posters printed without the wait or the markup of big print shops.

Whether it's a photo from a trip, a piece of digital art, a club flyer, or a poster for your team, we believe it should look as good on paper as it does on your screen. That's why we print on heavyweight paper, double-check every file before it goes to the printer, and only send out posters we'd hang ourselves.

Our mission

Make custom printing simple: upload a file, get a fair price, receive a pristine poster.
